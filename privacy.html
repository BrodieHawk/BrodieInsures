Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

This policy explains how BrodieInsures handles information through this website, including when you request an insurance quote, send a message, or submit a testimonial.

Information you provide

  • Quote requests. We collect your name, email, requested coverage type, and any phone number or message you provide. A homeowners quote request also asks for the property address.
  • Testimonials. We collect your name, email, optional city, rating, review text, and permission to publish.
  • Direct messages. If you contact Brodie by email, phone, or text, we receive your contact details and the information you choose to share.

Leave out Social Security numbers, payment information, and other sensitive information when using the website forms.

How we use and share information

We use quote and contact details to respond to your questions, follow up, and help you explore coverage. Information relevant to your request may be shared with insurance agencies or carriers when needed to obtain or discuss coverage options.

Testimonials are reviewed before publication. With your permission, an approved testimonial can display your first name, last initial, city if provided, rating, and review text. The email address you enter in the email field is kept private.

We use website hosting and maintenance providers, Supabase for form storage and administration, Resend for email notifications, and Formspree when a form uses its alternate submission route. These providers process information as needed to provide their services. A quote notification can contain the details you submitted.

Cookies and analytics

The site uses Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how people find and use it. Google may collect information such as pages visited, referral sources, browser and device details, and approximate location. Google Analytics can use cookies or similar identifiers to measure activity. Google provides more information about the data Analytics collects and an Analytics opt-out add-on for supported browsers.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. The site does not change its behavior in response to browser Do Not Track signals.

Privacy questions and requests

To ask what information you submitted, request a correction or deletion, or ask us to remove a published testimonial, email Brodie or use the contact details on this site. We may ask for enough information to find your submission and verify your request.

When we make material changes to this policy, we will post the revised policy here and update the date above.
