How I got started

I started working in insurance in 2024 and made property insurance my specialty. That includes homes, condos, and rental properties. Those conversations can look very different. Someone buying their first home might be juggling a closing date and a list of lender requirements. Someone renting out a former home might be figuring out what changes once a tenant moves in.

Working with people through those different stages has taught me to ask better questions up front. It’s useful to know what prompted the call, what’s changed since the last policy, and what someone wants to understand before making a decision.

What matters to me

I value being easy to reach and making time for questions. I like a straightforward conversation where we can talk through what’s going on before opening the policy. If a renewal surprised you or you’re seeing two very different prices for similar coverage, that’s a good place to start.

When we get into the details, I’ll explain the tradeoffs in plain language. We can look at what a deductible would mean in practice, where limits differ, and which exclusions are worth a closer look. I want you to be able to ask the same questions of any policy you’re considering.

Let’s talk.

If you’re planning a move, reviewing a renewal, or just have a question, you can reach me directly.
