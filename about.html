About Me

I’m Brodie Williams. I grew up in Granite Bay, California, and it’s still home. It’s also where I’ve based my insurance business. I work with people close to home in Granite Bay, Roseville, Rocklin, and Loomis, as well as clients across California.

I started working in insurance in 2024. I specialize in property insurance and work with people at different stages of owning a home, from buying a first place to renting out a house they used to live in. I also help families and business owners with auto and business insurance.

What it’s like to work with me

I start by asking what’s going on in your life and what brought you to me. You might be buying a home and working toward a closing date, or you might have opened your renewal and found a change you weren’t expecting. If you already have a policy, I’ll ask what you like about it and what you’d want to change.

I’ll walk through the options with you in plain language. That includes what you’d pay out of pocket after a covered loss, how much the policy could pay, and where there are gaps. I’ll make time for questions so you can decide which tradeoffs you’re comfortable with.

How I can help

  • Buying a home or condo. I’ll help you compare quotes before closing. We can discuss how the home’s features affect your options and which coverage details to confirm with your lender or condo association.
  • Insuring a rental property. If you’re buying an investment property or renting out a home you used to live in, I’ll help you look for coverage that reflects who lives there and how the property is used.
  • Finding coverage in a wildfire-prone area. If your home is in an area with elevated wildfire risk, I can help you explore insurance options beyond the California FAIR Plan. We’ll look at what’s available for your address and compare wildfire coverage, deductibles, and exclusions across the options you qualify for.
  • Reviewing a renewal or nonrenewal. If your premium goes up, your terms change, or you receive a nonrenewal notice, we can review the paperwork together. I’ll help you identify what changed, note any deadlines, and compare the available options.

What we’ll review together

For a house, we can talk through the estimated cost to rebuild, coverage for belongings, and what happens if a covered loss leaves you temporarily unable to live there. For a condo, we can review where the association’s coverage ends and where your own policy may need to pick up.

For a rental property, we can discuss the building, landlord liability, and whether the policy includes loss of rental income after a covered event. If you already have a policy, we can use it as a starting point to compare limits, deductibles, and exclusions.
