Meet your insurance agent

I’m Brodie Williams.

I’m an insurance agent with Placer West Insurance Services, based in Granite Bay. I work with families and business owners across California. My goal is to make insurance straightforward and give you time to ask questions about the coverage you’re considering.

Whether you’re buying your first home, adding a driver, or renewing a business policy, I’ll start by asking what you want to protect. Then we’ll look at the limits, deductibles, and exclusions together so you know what to expect from your policy.
