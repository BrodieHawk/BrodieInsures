Umbrella Insurance in Granite Bay, CA

I help California homeowners, drivers, and landlords review how much liability coverage they carry and whether a personal umbrella policy makes sense for their household.

How does umbrella insurance work?

A personal umbrella policy can provide additional liability limits above qualifying auto, homeowners, or landlord policies. If a covered claim exceeds the underlying policy’s liability limit, the umbrella may help pay the remaining covered amount, up to its own limit.

For example, after a serious auto accident for which you’re responsible, an umbrella policy may help if eligible injury claims exceed your auto liability limit. It generally won’t pay to repair your own car or home.

What should you check before choosing a policy?

  • Required liability limits. Umbrella insurers often require specific minimum limits on the policies underneath. We’ll check your current limits and see whether any need to change.
  • Who and what is included. We can review household drivers, vehicles, and rental properties. A rental property may need to be listed or covered under a qualifying landlord policy.
  • Exclusions and separate policies. Ask how the policy treats activities such as short-term rentals or business use of a vehicle. Commercial exposures may call for separate business coverage.
