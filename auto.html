Auto Insurance in California

I help drivers across California compare auto insurance. We can review how you use your vehicles and choose coverage limits and deductibles that fit your needs.

What can auto insurance cover?

Policies vary by insurer and by the coverages you select. Here are common options we can review.

  • Collision. Can help pay to repair or replace your vehicle after a covered collision.
  • Liability. Can help cover injury or property damage claims from others after an accident you cause.
    • Bodily injury liability applies to covered injury claims.
    • Property damage liability applies to covered damage to someone else’s vehicle or other property.
  • Medical payments (MedPay). Can help pay eligible medical expenses for you and your passengers after an accident.
  • Uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage. Can help cover certain losses if the at-fault driver has too little insurance or none at all.
  • Comprehensive. Can help pay for covered damage from theft, vandalism, hail, or other events outside of a collision.

We can also look at options such as rental reimbursement and whether bundling home and auto makes sense for your household.
