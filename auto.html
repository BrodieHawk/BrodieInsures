What to have handy

Start with the vehicles you drive and the people who drive them. If you’re comparing a policy, have its limits and deductibles handy.

What can auto insurance cover?

  • Liability. Helps cover eligible injury and property damage claims if you cause an accident.
  • Collision. Helps pay to repair or replace your car after a covered collision.
  • Comprehensive. Helps cover theft, vandalism, hail, and other covered damage outside a collision.
  • Uninsured and underinsured motorist. Can help cover certain losses when an at-fault driver has too little insurance or none.
  • Medical payments (MedPay). Can help with eligible medical expenses for you and your passengers after an accident.
