Coverage worth comparing

Liability
Helps cover eligible injury and property damage claims if you cause an accident.
Collision
Helps pay to repair or replace your car after a covered collision.
Comprehensive
Helps cover theft, vandalism, hail, and other covered damage outside a collision.
Uninsured and underinsured motorist
Can help cover certain losses when an at-fault driver has too little insurance or none.
Medical payments (MedPay)
Can help with eligible medical expenses for you and your passengers after an accident.
